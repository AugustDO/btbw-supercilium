combo 
Unique leg band combination of a female (three colorbands and one aluminum band issued from the National Bird Banding Laboratory; R = Red, O = Orange, Y = Yellow, G = Green, B = Blue, K = blacK, W = White, P = Pink, A = Aluminum). 


year
Year that the female was photographed and measured.


age
Age of the female (SY = second year, ASY = after second year).


tarsus 
Length of the right tarsus (mm).


nares 
Length of the bill from the nares to the bill tip (mm).


mass
Female mass (g).


plot	
The subplot where the female nested (N. Upper, S. Upper, Outer, Main, Mid, Lower).


elevation
The elevation zone where the female nested (high, middle, low).


s_r_area
Area of the right supercilium (mm²).


s_l_area
Area of the left supercilium (mm²).


u_r_area
Area of the right under-eye mark (mm²).


u_l_area
Area of the left under-eye mark (mm²).


s_r_bright
Brightness of the right supercilium (RGB).


s_l_bright
Brightness of the left supercilium (RGB).


u_r_bright
Brightness of the right under-eye mark (RGB).


u_l_bright
Brightness of the left under-eye mark (RGB).


lores_r_bright
Brightness of the right lores (RGB).


lores_l_bright
Brightness of the left lores (RGB).


r_cont
Difference in brightness between the right supercilium and lores (RGB).


l_cont
Difference in brightness between the left supercilium and lores (RGB).


complete_repro 
Whether all successful nests (fledged young) of a female were found on a territory in a single season (yes or no).


n_fledge
Number of young that fledged from a female's nest(s) in a single season.


cid
Ordinal date of the initiation (day first egg laid) of the first clutch of a female.


db_att
Whether the female attempted a second brood after her first brood fledged (yes or no).


db_succ
Whether the female successfully fledged a second brood after her first brood fledged (yes or no).


f_provision_rate
Number of female visits to the nest with food per nestling per hour (visits/nestling/hour).


f_prey_biomass
Amount of insect prey biomass (mg) delivered by a female to nestlings per nestling per hour (mg/nestling/hour).


f_nest_attentiveness
Duration of time the female spent at the nest during provisioning per nestling per hour (seconds/nestling/hour).


m_provision_rate
Number of male visits to the nest with food per nestling per hour (visits/nestling/hour).


m_prey_biomass
Amount of insect prey biomass (mg) delivered by a male to nestlings per nestlings per hour (mg/nestling/hour).


m_nest_attentiveness
Duration of time the male spent at the nest during provisioning per nestling per hour (seconds/nestling/hour).


polygynous
Whether a female’s social mate was simultaneously paired with a second female on the same territory (0 = monogamous, or 1 = polygynous).


food_availability
Estimate of food availability in territory (ln caterpillar biomass (mg).
